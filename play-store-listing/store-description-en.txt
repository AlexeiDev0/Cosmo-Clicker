Space Salvage

Short description:
Collect space salvage, upgrade your drones and discover new planets.

Full description:
Turn space debris into the start of a growing expedition. In Space Salvage, you collect salvage, build a drone fleet and unlock new planets. Begin by gathering resources manually, develop automatic collection and decide where your next reward belongs: better equipment, another drone or the next journey.

This single-player space clicker combines gradual progression, collections and passive income. Play actively to gather resources, or return for short sessions to check your fleet and continue exploring.

BUILD YOUR DRONE FLEET
Discover 41 drone models, from your first collectors to epic and legendary machines. Recruit drones, choose your active fleet and send it out for salvage. Drone rarity helps determine which valuable finds it can collect. Visit the hangar to track your collection, launch or recall drones and sell unwanted units.

FUSE RESERVE DRONES
Combine two healthy reserve drones of the same rarity for a chance to receive a rarer drone. The workshop shows the chance before you confirm. Both drones are consumed, including when fusion fails.

DISCOVER 48 SALVAGE TYPES
Hull fragments, ship modules, batteries, scanner lenses, quantum prisms and supernova cores all have a place in your collection. Find common, uncommon, rare, epic and legendary items. Each newly discovered type fills another collection slot. Gather different types to complete quests and earn discovery rewards.

EXPLORE A ROUTE OF 39 PLANETS
Unlock new worlds and travel further along the galactic route. Your next destination requires resources and preparation. Planet conditions change your expedition, while route requirements give you a clear goal: improve collection, strengthen your fleet and reach the next world.

UPGRADE YOUR EQUIPMENT AND STATION
Start with a plasma magnet and work towards powerful technologies. Improve manual collection, expand your fleet's capabilities and choose upgrades that suit your pace. Develop your hangar, laboratory, reactor and trade hub for additional expedition benefits.

REACT TO SPACE EVENTS
Meteor showers, trading ships, pirate raids, distress signals and abandoned stations bring new situations to your journey. Collect extra salvage, make decisions and restore drones after incidents. Follow the event log to see how your expedition unfolds.

COMPLETE QUESTS AND ACHIEVEMENTS
Daily and weekly quests provide goals for quick visits and longer sessions. Collect resources and different finds, develop your fleet and claim rewards. Open cases with earned in-game resources for random game rewards.

START ANOTHER CYCLE WITH PRESTIGE
Begin a new stage of progression with permanent upgrades. Prestige helps you revisit familiar worlds faster and travel further. Discovered collections carry over between prestige cycles.

PLAY AT YOUR OWN PACE
The core game works offline. Offline income is calculated for up to eight hours away and awarded when you return. When an ad is available, you can choose to watch it to double that reward. You receive the regular reward without watching.

Clean drone silhouettes, calm colors and a minimal visual style keep the focus on your expedition. Adjust sound and reduced motion to your preference. English, Russian and Spanish are supported, and progress is stored on your device.

There are no real-money purchases, cash prizes or cash withdrawals.

Start with your first piece of salvage, build your fleet and discover the next planet in Space Salvage.
