# Fleet income progression — 11 October 2026

Fleet productivity now grows with permanent click upgrades. The multiplier is `max(1, base upgraded click / 25)`, where base upgraded click is one plus the purchased upgrade contributions, before combos, random critical hits, events, station bonuses and temporary skills. Existing drone rarity, planet, technology, mastery and collection multipliers remain in the live income calculation.

For example, six common drones formerly earned 30 debris/second on the starting planet. At a base upgraded click of 1,000, the same fleet now earns 1,200/second before other bonuses. No drones still means no fleet income. Buying more drones and obtaining rarer ones still increases income.

The same progression multiplier also applies to offline earnings, retaining the existing offline efficiency, duration cap and lower offline rarity rates. Shop advertising rewards use the updated live fleet quote, so their economy scaling remains consistent. Existing saves benefit immediately without a migration.
