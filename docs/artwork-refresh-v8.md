Final prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: portrait 2:3 main background for a flat vector style space salvage mobile game. Make a cohesive clean illustrated space scene matching game objects drawn with thick dark navy outlines, pale slate metal bodies, teal glass drone cores, pastel blue planets, warm cream and gold rare scrap. Background must be dark midnight navy, with crisp simple stylized small stars, flat layered blue planet crescent at the lower outer corner, small angular asteroid fragments near edges, subtle thin orbit arcs. Restrained teal and slate colors with a few muted gold accents. Flat cel shading, precise smooth shapes, no photographic or painterly texture, no fog, no busy nebulas. Keep central 75 percent quiet and dark to display existing moving drones, scrap, planet and UI; do not draw any drones, UI, text or logos. Background only.

The 48 debris vectors retain stable discovery IDs and silhouettes. Their new v8 resources add metallic shading and material highlights. Upgrade/system icons use individual v3 vectors. Eight drones were added: six ordinary case drops and two merchant exclusives. Merchant Manta appears on approximately 4% of visits; Caravan Crown on approximately 2%. The parts barter appears only in the rare merchant assortment.

English, Russian and Spanish drone names use localized arrays. Six additional languages translate core controls only; remaining strings fall back to English. System language stays first in the chooser.
