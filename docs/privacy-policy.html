Space Salvage — privacy policy Version 11 · 19 October 2026 Local data and purpose The game stores resources, planets, drones, upgrades, quests, achievements, gameplay statistics and activity timestamps to save progress, calculate offline rewards and schedule challenges. Language, sound and reduced-motion preferences, and whether the introductory tutorial has been completed or skipped, are also stored locally. Tap timing and positions are temporarily analysed on the device to detect automated tapping; that temporary history is not sent to the developer. Local input protection The game also stores temporary planet-tap cooldowns, the number and time of recent detections, and the Android boot counter locally to preserve a cooldown across an app restart. This metadata contains no tap coordinates or history, is not sent to the developer and is excluded from backups. The boot counter distinguishes device restarts; it is not an advertising identifier. A cooldown lasts at most two minutes; repeated-detection escalation expires after five quiet minutes. Clearing app storage removes this metadata. Network and third parties The app includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for optional rewarded advertising and advertising privacy choices. There are no player accounts or real-money purchases. Google may process IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from IP), device and advertising identifiers where available, interactions with the app and ads (such as app launches, taps and video views), and diagnostic data to deliver ads, prevent fraud and measure performance. Advertising requests are treated as below the age of consent for the whole 13+ audience; this restricts personalization, but does not mean that no data is processed. Debug builds use Google demo ads and earn no advertising revenue. Release builds use production advertising identifiers for optional rewarded ads. The game does not send saved progress or tap history to the developer or advertising SDK. It does not request access to contacts, precise location, camera or microphone. Vibration permission provides touch feedback. Google data practices: https://policies.google.com/privacy . Android backups Android may back up game progress and settings or transfer them to another device, depending on your system and backup-provider settings. These copies are controlled by the platform/provider, not by the game developer. Manage them in Android backup settings. Consent is excluded from app backups, so it is requested again after a restore or transfer. Retention and deletion Progress remains until you clear app storage in Android settings. Clearing storage deletes all local game data, settings, consent and input-protection state. Uninstalling removes local app data, but existing system backups may remain; manage them separately with your backup provider. Your choice Before agreement, the game does not create the game session or load/save progress. Agreement is stored locally with its date and policy version. You can reopen this text and withdraw in Settings. Withdrawal stops further gameplay processing but does not erase your save or platform backups. To play again, agree again. Questions Developer: Alexei Fitlin. Privacy contact: alex.fitlin@gmail.com. Opening the link does not automatically send game data. If data practices change, this policy must be updated and consent requested for the new version. Security and support Progress and settings are kept in private Android app storage. The game does not add encryption to these files; device and backup protection depend on Android and your settings. Bundled music and sounds play locally. When you email support, the recipient receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Messages are used to answer your request; request deletion through the same email. Your email provider handles its own copies. Do not send passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information. Advertising rewards and choices Watching an ad is optional; ordinary offline income is credited without advertising. The game stores a pending bonus identifier, amount and duration alongside progress to prevent duplicate rewards. The bonus is granted only after the advertising SDK confirms that it was earned. Production ad requests are checked against UMP; when required, Advertising privacy choices are available in Settings. Reading or accepting this policy is not consent to personalized advertising. UMP may store advertising privacy choices on the device. Closing the game prevents further ad requests by the game; it does not delete data already processed by Google. Manage Google advertising choices at https://myadcenter.google.com/ and contact Google about data it processes. Optional advertising requests The game does not automatically preload rewarded ads. Tapping a button explicitly labelled Watch ad allows Google advertising requests for this app session and starts loading and showing an ad without a separate game confirmation. Before a shop reward, the displayed notice explains that Google loads the ad. UMP is accessed after that tap and may display any required Google privacy form. Without tapping an advertising button, the game does not request an ad. Settings lets you stop new advertising requests; this cannot undo an in-flight request, stop all processing inside an already initialized Google SDK, or delete data already sent to Google. Permission is not saved across app restarts or Activity recreation. Accepting the game privacy policy alone does not allow advertising requests. The advertising ID and Privacy Sandbox advertising permissions are explicitly removed from the app manifest; other available SDK identifiers may still be processed. Watching ads is optional. Shop rewards give at least 5,000 debris for one confirmed rewarded view, with the amount scaled to unlocked planets and fleet income, or one common case for three confirmed rewarded views. The game saves the case-view counter and pending reward identifier, type and quoted amount locally to prevent duplicate rewards. Closing an ad without earning its reward does not count. Rewarded bonuses are not cash. Cases can be obtained with earned game debris or rewarded ads. There are no real-money purchases. Bundled artwork and language options Backgrounds, drone and debris illustrations, icons and fonts are bundled with the app and displayed locally. The game does not contact image-generation or translation services when you play or change language. System language follows your device settings; a manual language choice is stored locally. Some languages currently translate only the main controls, with other text falling back to English. New drone types and collection rarity groups use the same locally stored game progress. Active play time The game stores the cumulative time spent in active gameplay locally to unlock time achievements. Time away from the app and rewarded-ad playback do not count. Player titles are calculated locally from claimed achievements. These counters are not sent to the developer. Drone fusion Fusion spends two healthy reserve drones of the same rarity. The success chance is shown before confirmation. Both drones are consumed; success adds one random drone of the target rarity and failure gives no reward. Fusion does not involve real-money purchases, cash prizes or a required advertising view. The resulting fleet inventory, drone discoveries and quest progress are stored in the existing local save. No separate history of fusion attempts is saved. The selected pair and animation state exist only in the current workshop window; closing it does not undo an already confirmed outcome. Selection and fusion do not themselves initiate advertising requests or send the pair or outcome to the developer. The animation is drawn locally, with reduced-motion preferences applied. The backup and deletion rules described above also apply to the updated inventory.

La flota resultante, los descubrimientos de drones y el progreso de misiones se guardan en la partida local existente. No se guarda un historial separado de intentos de fusión. La pareja elegida y el estado de la animación solo existen en la ventana actual del taller; cerrarla no deshace un resultado ya confirmado. Elegir o fusionar drones no inicia por sí mismo solicitudes publicitarias ni envía la pareja o el resultado al desarrollador. La animación se dibuja localmente y respeta el ajuste de movimiento reducido. Las reglas de copia de seguridad y eliminación anteriores también se aplican a la flota actualizada.
