Drona Salvage — privacy policy Version 3 · 3 October 2026 Local data and purpose The game stores resources, planets, drones, upgrades, quests, achievements, gameplay statistics and activity timestamps to save progress, calculate offline rewards and schedule challenges. Language, sound and reduced-motion preferences are also stored locally. Tap timing and positions are temporarily analysed on the device to detect automated tapping; that temporary history is not sent to the developer. Local input protection The game also stores temporary planet-tap cooldowns, the number and time of recent detections, and the Android boot counter locally to preserve a cooldown across an app restart. This metadata contains no tap coordinates or history, is not sent to the developer and is excluded from backups. The boot counter distinguishes device restarts; it is not an advertising identifier. A cooldown is at most two minutes, repeated-detection escalation expires after five quiet minutes, and resetting progress does not clear it. Clearing app storage removes this metadata. Network and third parties This version has no accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs or in-game real-money payments. The game does not send your save or tap history to the developer and does not access contacts, location, camera or microphone. Vibration permission provides touch feedback. Choosing the Telegram contact opens an external service; information you choose to send there is handled by Telegram and the recipient. Do not send sensitive information. Android backups Android may back up game progress and settings or transfer them to another device, depending on your system and backup-provider settings. These copies are controlled by the platform/provider, not by the game developer. Manage them in Android backup settings. Consent is excluded from app backups, so it is requested again after a restore or transfer. Retention and deletion Local progress remains until you reset it or clear app storage. Reset game progress deletes the saved game and creates a new starting save; language and other preferences remain. Android Clear storage removes all local app data. Local deletion does not guarantee removal of existing system backups; manage those separately with your provider. Your choice Before agreement, the game does not create the game session or load/save progress. Agreement is stored locally with its date and policy version. You can reopen this text and withdraw in Settings. Withdrawal stops further gameplay processing but does not erase your save or platform backups. To play again, agree again. The game does not provide additional encryption of these files; device security and system backups depend on Android and your settings. All music and sound effects are bundled with the app and are played locally without contacting asset websites. If you contact the developer through Telegram, the recipient can see the profile and messages that you share. Support messages are used to answer your request; request their deletion through the same contact. Telegram handles its own copies under its privacy policy. Drona Salvage — política de privacidad Versión 3 · 3 de octubre de 2026 Datos locales y finalidad El juego guarda recursos, planetas, drones, mejoras, misiones, logros, estadísticas y fechas de actividad para conservar el progreso, calcular recompensas sin conexión y renovar misiones. También guarda idioma, sonido y movimiento reducido. Analiza temporalmente los tiempos y posiciones de pulsaciones en el dispositivo para detectar clics automáticos; este historial no se envía al desarrollador. Protección local de pulsaciones El juego también guarda pausas temporales de pulsaciones del planeta, el número y la fecha de detecciones recientes y el contador de arranques de Android para conservar la pausa al reiniciar la aplicación. No guarda posiciones ni historial de pulsaciones en estos datos, no los envía al desarrollador y los excluye de las copias. El contador distingue reinicios del dispositivo; no es un identificador publicitario. La pausa dura como máximo dos minutos; la escalada caduca tras cinco minutos tranquilos. Restablecer el progreso no elimina la pausa. Borrar el almacenamiento de la aplicación elimina estos datos. Las controla la plataforma o el proveedor, no el desarrollador del juego. Adminístralas en los ajustes de copias de Android.
